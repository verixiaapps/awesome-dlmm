# Awesome DLMM [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of tools, analytics, and resources for DLMM (Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker) liquidity providers and traders on Solana — Meteora DLMM, DAMM, and the ecosystem around them.

DLMM pools concentrate liquidity into discrete price bins, which makes LP positions far more capital-efficient — and far more active to manage — than classic constant-product AMMs. This list collects the tooling that has grown around that job: analytics, automation, alerts, and education.

Contributions welcome — see [Contributing](#contributing).

## Liquidity Analytics & Intelligence

- [CLOBr](https://clobr.io) — Aggregated liquidity depth for any Solana token: combines DLMM/DEX pool liquidity, on-chain DCA orders, and CEX order books (40+ sources) into real support/resistance levels, whale order tracking, and liquidity alerts.
- [Rocket Scan](https://rocketscan.fun) — Discover early pool opportunities and capture fees as a Meteora LP.
- [Tokleo](https://tokleo.com) — Real-time analytics for Meteora pools enhanced with on-chain data.
- [Metlab](https://metlab.app) — Real-time analytics and monitoring for Meteora DLMM & DAMM v2 pools.
- [Fabriq](https://fabriq.trade) — Trading platform with precision tools and real-time liquidity insights.
- [Metlex](https://metlex.io) — Supporting toolkit for the Meteora DLMM journey.

## Position Management & Automation

- [MetEngine](https://metengine.xyz) — Create and manage DLMM and DAMM positions from Telegram, web, and Farcaster.
- [HawkFi](https://hawkfi.ag) — LP automation for Meteora, Orca, and Raydium (rebalancing, compounding).
- [Milk Money](https://milkmoney.trade) — LP automation terminal.
- [Cleopetra](https://cleopetra.io) — Operating system for on-chain liquidity provisioning.
- [Decoder Farmer](https://decoder-1.gitbook.io) — Automated Meteora DLMM farmer with social/copy-trading via Discord.
- [Liquid Nova](https://liquidnova.app) — Custom automated strategies with real-time monitoring and P&L tracking.
- [DAMMIT](https://dammit.pro/positions) — Monitor Meteora DAMM pool health and execute maintenance.
- [UnifAI Network](https://unifai.network) — AI-native infrastructure to automate strategy execution.
